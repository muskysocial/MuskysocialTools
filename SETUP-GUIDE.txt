MUSKYSOCIALS & TOOLS — FREE WEBSITE STARTER

1. Open index.html in a browser to preview the site.

2. In index.html, find:
   const WHATSAPP_NUMBER = "234XXXXXXXXXX";

   Replace it with your WhatsApp number in international format.
   Example:
   const WHATSAPP_NUMBER = "2348012345678";

3. Find the "listings" section and replace the sample accounts with your real listings.
   Each listing has:
   - id
   - platform
   - title
   - followers
   - niche
   - audience
   - price
   - status

4. The website DOES NOT collect payment.
   Customers click "Order on WhatsApp", discuss the order and payment with you,
   and you manually confirm payment before delivering anything.

FREE HOSTING OPTIONS
- GitHub Pages: upload index.html to a public repository and enable Pages.
- Netlify: upload the folder through its free deployment options.
- Cloudflare Pages: connect/upload the project on its free tier.

IMPORTANT
- Do not list stolen, hacked, compromised, or fraudulently obtained accounts.
- Check the rules of each social platform regarding account transfers/sales.
- Keep proof of ownership and order records.
